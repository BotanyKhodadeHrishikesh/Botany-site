Educational Qualifications & Academic Achievements

Strong academic background in Life Sciences and Botany with advanced qualifications in biological sciences, competitive national-level examinations, and specialized research training in Green Nanotechnology and interdisciplinary biological research.

  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Botany — Completed in 2009 from Modern College of Arts, Science and Commerce, Shivajinagar, Pune, with foundational training in plant sciences, microbiology, genetics, ecology, and biological research methodologies.
  • Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Botany — Completed in 2011 from Department of Botany, SPPU, Pune, with specialization in advanced biological sciences including plant biotechnology, molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, ecology, and environmental biology.
  • UGC-SET (Life Sciences) — Qualified in 2011, - Academic competency and eligibility for teaching at higher education level in the field of Life Sciences.
  • CSIR-NET-JRF (Life Sciences) — Qualified in 2012, - One of India’s prestigious national-level competitive examinations for research fellowship and higher scientific research in Life Sciences.
  • GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) — Qualified in 2012, - strong analytical and scientific aptitude in interdisciplinary biological and applied science subjects.
  • Doctoral Research (Ph.D.) in Green Nanotechnology — Research focused on plant-mediated synthesis of nanoparticles, nanobiotechnology, phytochemical investigations, and evaluation of biological activities including anticancer, antioxidant, antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antidiabetic applications.

Research Skills & Technical Expertise

Nanotechnology & Nanoparticle Characterization

Specialized in the green synthesis and characterization of metallic and metal oxide nanoparticles using plant-mediated approaches, with particular expertise in eco-friendly nanotechnology and biological applications of nanoparticles.

  • Extensive experience in biogenic synthesis of Silver (Ag), Zinc Oxide (ZnO), Copper (Cu), Iron (Fe), Nickel (Ni), and Cobalt (Co) nanoparticles using plant extracts.
  • Skilled in optimization of nanoparticle synthesis parameters including precursor concentration, pH, temperature, incubation time, and stabilization techniques.
  • Hands-on expertise in nanoparticle characterization using advanced analytical techniques such as UV-Visible Spectroscopy, Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR), X-Ray Diffraction (XRD), Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy (FESEM), Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS), and Zeta Potential analysis.
  • Experienced in interpreting spectroscopic, crystallographic, morphological, and particle size distribution data for nanoparticle validation and quality assessment.
  • Research focus on the biomedical and pharmacological applications of nanoparticles including anticancer, antioxidant, antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antidiabetic activities.
  • Knowledge of nanomaterial synthesis protocols, laboratory handling procedures, data interpretation, and scientific documentation for research publications and thesis preparation.
  • Capable of integrating green nanotechnology approaches with medicinal plant research for sustainable and biologically active nanomaterial development.

Biological, Phytochemical & Experimental Research Studies

Experienced in multidisciplinary biological research involving phytochemical investigations, bioactivity assessment, environmental studies, and laboratory-based experimental analysis with a strong focus on medicinal plant research and nanobiotechnology.

  • Proficient in qualitative and quantitative phytochemical analysis of medicinal plant extracts for identification of biologically active compounds.
  • Hands-on experience in antioxidant activity evaluation using standard free radical scavenging assays and comparative bioactivity studies.
  • Skilled in antimicrobial research including antibacterial activity assessment against pathogenic microorganisms using standard microbiological techniques.
  • Experienced in anticancer research using MTT assay for evaluating cytotoxic effects of plant extracts and synthesized nanoparticles on cancer cell lines.
  • Knowledge of plant sample processing, extract preparation, tissue handling, and laboratory protocols for biological experimentation.
  • Worked on environmental and ecological studies including water pollution assessment, dye degradation analysis, and seed germination experiments under laboratory conditions.
  • Capable of experimental design, scientific data interpretation, graphical representation, and statistical analysis for research validation and publication purposes.
  • Familiar with interdisciplinary research approaches integrating phytochemistry, microbiology, environmental science, and nanotechnology.
